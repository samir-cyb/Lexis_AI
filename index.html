Features

Everything you need for effective, engaging speech therapy — right in your browser.

🎙️

Real-Time Audio Engine

Capture your voice with zero-lag browser microphone interface. See your sound waves and spectrograms live as you speak.

🎮

Phonetic Game Sandbox

Interactive exercises where your voice controls the game. Speak phrases to move characters, clear hurdles, and earn rewards.

🧠

Adaptive Curriculum

AI tracks which sounds cause difficulty and automatically adjusts exercise difficulty to keep you in the optimal learning zone.

🧒

Kid-Friendly Mode

A magical, colorful interface designed for young learners under 10. Cute characters, fun sounds, and rewarding animations.

🌐

Bangla & English

Full support for both Bangla and English phonemes. Practice in either language with localized exercises and interfaces.

📊

Progress Tracking

Detailed analytics showing your improvement over time. Track phonemic clarity scores and celebrate milestones.

How It Works

Three simple steps to start your speech therapy journey.

1

Sign Up & Set Up

Create your account, select your age group, preferred language, and practice duration. Our system adapts to you from the start.

2

Practice with Games

Speak into your microphone to control interactive games. Your voice navigates characters, matches words, and chains vowels through fun challenges.

3

Track & Improve

Our AI analyzes your performance in real-time, identifying weak phonemes and automatically adjusting difficulty to maximize your improvement.
